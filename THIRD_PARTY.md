# Third-party components and model rights

The project AGPL-3.0-only license does not grant rights to books, voices, upstream engines or model weights. Weights are not bundled. Check the exact revision's terms before downloading, redistributing or commercial use. This is a navigation aid, not legal advice or an exhaustive license inventory.
